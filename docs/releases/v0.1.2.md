# v0.1.2 - Non-Color Float Change Markers

This patch release improves reviewer-facing readability for whole table and
figure changes.

## New

- Whole newly added tables and figures now receive an underlined visible label,
  such as `[Added table]` or `[Added figure]`.
- Whole deleted tables and figures whose bodies are commented out by `latexdiff`
  now receive a struck visible label, such as `[Deleted table]`.
- Table/float reports now describe whole additions and deletions rather than
  only whole table additions.

## Why

Color alone is not a reliable review marker: it is weak in grayscale printing,
can be missed in dense tables, and is less accessible. The generated manuscript
now combines color with underlined/struck labels while avoiding fragile
underlining around entire `tabular` bodies.
